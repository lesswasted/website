LESS WASTED

BETTER DRINKS. LESS WASTAGE

Turning what's often wasted into delicious, low alcohol fermented drinks, so you get less wasted

1
We rescue
fruit peels
We rescue fresh organic fruit peels that would otherwise go to waste and ferment them into tasty, healthy drinks. In the case of lemon and lime peel this leaves a product that is also improved and can be used in dishes.
→
2
We ferment
naturally
We ferment in the traditional way with real cultures over many days, just like people have been doing for hundreds and even thousands of years. No shortcuts, or modified cultures. just real organic ingredients.
→
3
Full of flavour,
not sugar
The sugar is mostly fermented away, creating depth, natural bubbles and complex flavours and <0.2g/100ml of sugar. We use Demarera Sugar, which is less refined than most, retaining trace minerals such as Iron, Chromium, Calcium and Potassium. Not enough to meet your RDA but the cultures love it!
4
Good for
your gut
Our drinks are naturally fermented and contain good bacteria that your gut will thank you for. They also produce their own CO2 negating the need to use artificially produced gas.
→
5
Better for
the planet
Less waste, less sugar, less transport. More flavour, less impact.
→
6
Made to be
enjoyed
For everyday moments, good conversations and people who care about what they drink.
